Great Career Advice from Dara Khosrowshahi (Uber CEO)

20 Jan 2023 · Francis Awagu

On a productive Friday morning working from my home office in Stockholm Sweden, I bumped into this great interview with Ryan Roslansky speaking with Dara the current CEO of Uber

This post is a summary of my personal take ways from his interview:

  1. Have an idea of where you want to go
  2. Be open to opportunity
  3. do not over plan your career eg being fixated on wanting to be a VP
  4. As humans, we tend to ignore signals in the opposite direction when our ideas about the future are too narrow.
  5. Un-familiar path can lead to a rewarding place
  6. Don’t be too much in a hurry. Greatness takes time and it takes time to build your craft. “career compounding is magical”
